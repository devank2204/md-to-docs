# Math and Equation Fidelity

Einstein's mass-energy equivalence is represented as $E = mc^2$ within inline text.

Here is the quadratic formula displayed as a standalone equation:

$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Euler's identity is $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

Here is another display equation with summation:

$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$$
